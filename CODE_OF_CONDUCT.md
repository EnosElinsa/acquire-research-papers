# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies to issues, pull requests, discussions in review threads, and any other project space, and to anyone who represents the project elsewhere.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent; disagree about code and evidence, not about people.
- Keep reports and reviews concrete: the command, the observed result, and the expected result.
- Protect other people's privacy. Never post someone's credentials, institution account, browser session, or private research data, and do not ask others to share theirs.
- Respect publisher terms and institutional licenses. Requests to bypass access controls, share paywalled papers, or automate prohibited publisher interactions are out of scope and will be closed.

Harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer through [GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/EnosElinsa/acquire-research-papers/security/advisories/new) with a title that starts with "Conduct report", or use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature for abusive content. Include links to the relevant comments. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and may edit or remove content, warn a participant, or block a participant from the repository, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant. A decision can be appealed through the same private channel.
